Who We Are

FlyOne Investment Corporation

FIC is a business group – financial group with optimal efficiency priorities in investment and business development strategies.

Founded in 2021 and headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City, FlyOne Investment Corporation has rapidly grown into a diversified conglomerate spanning aviation solutions, travel, real estate, technology, cargo logistics, and mobility services.

Our business philosophy: "People are important assets & social prosperity is our business philosophy." We build lasting value for shareholders, employees, customers, and the broader community.

To be a leading business group and financial group with optimal efficiency priorities in investment and business development — achieving a 10-time increase in capitalization by 2025, with a nationwide branch network from Ho Chi Minh City, Ha Noi, Da Nang, Da Lat, Phu Quoc, Nha Trang to Hai Phong and beyond internationally.

Our Mission

  1. Providing specific products and services with professionalism and high service quality to customers.
  2. Developing sustainably; increasing material and spiritual value for our members.
  3. Ensuring efficient use of capital for investors and shareholders.
  4. Contributing the best values to society and to the community.

Core Business Areas

FIC deploys capital and expertise across six major verticals, each driving the ecosystem forward.

Core Business

Real Estate & Securities

Strategic financial investment and real estate across Vietnam. Investing in the FIC ecosystem chain including Airport Ground Handling, Air Support Services, Education, Healthcare, Transport, and Construction.

  • Financial & real estate investment
  • Securities trading
  • M&A strategies (M&ASTEPS)

Aviation Solutions

One-stop aviation services as an IATA Accredited Agent with partnerships across major airlines in Vietnam and the region.

  • Cargo & Passenger GSA
  • B2B Ticketing Platform (1,000+ sub-agents)
  • Air Charter Center
  • Airport Supervision & Services

Travel & Tourism

Luxury and corporate travel solutions through Travel One, Vietnam's first air charter hub, with high-end tours and executive travel services.

  • Luxury & VIP tours
  • Corporate & MICE travel
  • Charter flights (private jet / large aircraft)
  • Wellness & discovery tours

Technology & IT Solutions

Building the digital backbone of the FIC ecosystem through Flytech — providing technology infrastructure and integrated IT solutions for all member companies.

  • Technology infrastructure
  • Integrated IT solutions
  • Software services
  • Digitalization systems

Cargo & Logistics

FAS Cargo Vietnam provides specialized cargo GSA services and shipping routes, expanding Vietnam's cargo capabilities internationally.
